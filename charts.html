Calibrate on your own controls (optional)

The published curves are those of the average training site. Sites differ, so a recording from a new site carries an unknown offset. Upload controls from your own site and the page estimates that offset and shifts the curves accordingly.
CSV with four columns, marker,value,age,sex, one row per control and per measure. The estimate is shrunk toward zero when few controls are available, so a small sample moves the curves little rather than moving them wrongly. Bethlehem et al. recommend at least 100 controls for this step.
